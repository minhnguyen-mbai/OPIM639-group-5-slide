The solution

One AI agent. Four steps.

A vision-capable foundation model, Refash's guidelines via RAG, a rules engine for payout policy, and a sell-through model trained on Refash's resale records.

02 · FairQuote · Minh

Photograph on receipt

On receipt, staff photograph each item and its label under a standard setup and give it a unique ID. FairQuote reads condition, material and brand, flags defects or missing information for a person to verify, and scores its confidence in each finding. Physical inspection stays compulsory.

Route by risk

Not every item needs the same attention. High-confidence, low-value items get a quick confirmation. Low-confidence, premium and disputed items get a full manual review.

Recommend, then decide

FairQuote recommends accept or reject and a payout amount, from Refash's evaluation criteria, its payout estimator and a sell-through prediction trained on Refash's own resale records. Staff confirm or override every recommendation, and every override is recorded with a reason.
Human judgment — most criticalStaff check every item by hand and decide. Changing a recommendation needs a recorded reason. Designer bags and disputes always go to a person.

03 · Pilot · Rocky

Testing FairQuote: a 4-week pilot before any roll-out

Run side by side with today's process, on womenswear (about 98% of Refash's online listings)

WEEKS 1-2

Staff test

Two experienced graders assess the same items separately: one using FairQuote, one the usual way.

We time every step, including photos, to measure real efficiency gains.

WEEKS 3-4

Seller test

Sellers are split into two similar groups. Group A gets a FairQuote itemised report; Group B gets the usual quote.

Independent graders set final payouts using the same policy for both groups.

Pilot budget: S$53,000 (S$15,000 to build + S$38,000 to run the pilot)

How we will decide whether FairQuote works

1

Speed

Time to assess each item, including photos

2

Cost

Staff hours and labour cost per item

3

Consistency

Do graders value similar items the same way?

4

Seller trust

Do sellers understand their quote? Are there fewer disputes?

Our go / no-go rule

Expand beyond womenswear only if FairQuote is accurate, cuts time per item, and sellers understand their quotes better.

Our planning estimate is a 20% cut in evaluation time. The pilot tests that assumption before any spending on wider roll-out.

Estimates from the Group 5 report; the pilot will test them.
